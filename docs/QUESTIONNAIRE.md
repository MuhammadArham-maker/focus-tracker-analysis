# Focus Tracker — Questionnaire

This document reproduces the final questionnaire used for the self-tracking experiment. It is written here for reading; it does not link to the private Google Forms editing or analytics pages.

## Form description

**Log the Focus. The Stillness. The Presence. Ignore the outcome.**

**Macro-blocks only.**  
(Example: Study + 5min Calisthenics break = 1 Study Log). Only submit a new log when you completely switch your main activity.

**The 10-Second Gut Check Rule.**

When you open those checkboxes, you are not allowed to debate with yourself. You read the list, and whatever your gut instantly says "Yes" to, you check.

- If you have to think for 60 seconds about whether you were in a "Flow State," you weren't. Leave it blank.
- Check the boxes that scream at you, hit submit, and put the phone down.

All four questions below were required.

## 3. Focus Density!

*Linear scale — mark only one oval.*

The scale ran from wandering (1) to **Pure Presence / Flow state** (5), as described in the project notes and shown by the form's endpoint label.

## Notes on the final form

- The original planning notes began with three questions. The final form had four because a time-block question was added.
- Activity and time block were single-choice questions.
- The driver question allowed multiple selections.
- Google Forms also recorded a submission timestamp automatically; it was not one of the four visible questions.
